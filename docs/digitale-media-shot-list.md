# DIGITALE — Cinematic Media Shot List

## Creative rule
Build one coherent visual world: premium, cinematic, editorial, human, technology-aware. No generic stock-agency look. No Urbanrise references.

## Hero showreel — 12 seconds
1. 0.0–1.5s — abstract macro detail / light / material / motion
2. 1.5–3.0s — brand identity system, typography and campaign composition
3. 3.0–4.5s — performance/growth dashboards, search, audience and conversion signals
4. 4.5–6.0s — product/web/app interaction on real devices
5. 6.0–7.5s — AI/automation visual language, subtle and editorial
6. 7.5–9.0s — production/camera/content creation
7. 9.0–10.5s — premium corporate launch / live experience / crowd energy
8. 10.5–12.0s — clean DIGITALE end frame with negative space

## Selected Work
- consumer-brand.webp: premium consumer campaign world; no identifiable third-party brand
- entertainment-launch.webp: cinematic launch/event atmosphere
- digital-product.webp: polished product/interface environment

## Experiences
- experience-hero.avif: premium live event / launch environment with architectural lighting and human energy
- Keep it usable across corporate events, launches, celebrations and entertainment.

## Founders
- uma-saravana-kumar.webp: editorial founder portrait, clean background, natural expression
- siva-veerapaneni.webp: matching editorial founder portrait
- Both should feel like one photographed set.

## Generation / production constraints
- 16:9 hero and work imagery unless a slot specifies otherwise.
- Avoid logos, fake client names, fake metrics, fake testimonials and fabricated case-study evidence.
- Avoid obvious AI artifacts: distorted hands, unreadable interface text, duplicate people, impossible architecture.
- Keep faces and brands generic unless an approved real reference image is supplied.
- Favor cinematic lighting, realistic materials, restrained motion blur and strong composition.
- Export optimized AVIF/WebP assets according to docs/asset-manifest.json.
